Our Story

A legacy of excellence

Nestled in the heart of the city, Le Château has been a beacon of gastronomic artistry for nearly a century. Our kitchen is a canvas where tradition meets innovation, guided by an unwavering commitment to quality and seasonality.

Every ingredient is carefully sourced from local artisans and small farms, ensuring that each dish captures the pure essence of its origin. We believe that dining is not just about food — it is an experience to be savoured, shared, and remembered.

— Chef Auguste Renard
